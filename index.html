Hi, I'm Jai Nandan.

Data Scientist | Data Analyst | AI/ML Enthusiast

I turn complex, messy, and often overlooked data into clear insights, predictive intelligence, and practical digital solutions. My work goes beyond simply training a machine learning model—I focus on understanding the problem behind the data, uncovering meaningful patterns, preparing reliable datasets, engineering useful features, and developing models that can support real-world decisions. From exploratory data analysis and statistical understanding to machine learning, visualization, evaluation, and deployment, I approach each project as an end-to-end journey where data is transformed into something useful, explainable, and actionable.

I enjoy building solutions that connect data, technology, and real-world impact—whether that means predicting customer behavior, identifying unusual patterns, forecasting outcomes, analyzing business performance, or developing intelligent applications. My goal is to create data-driven systems that are not only technically sound, but also understandable, reproducible, scalable, and valuable to the people who use them.
