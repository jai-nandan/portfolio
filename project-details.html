Problem Statement

The goal is to identify at-risk customers early enough for a retention action to be considered before the customer leaves.

Objective

The goal is to identify at-risk customers early enough for a retention action to be considered before the customer leaves.

Dataset

IBM Telco Customer Churn dataset containing customer demographics, account information, services, billing details and churn labels.

Data Preparation

Handled missing and inconsistent values, corrected data types, encoded categorical variables and prepared the dataset for modeling.

Data Cleaning & EDA

Handled missing and inconsistent values, corrected data types, encoded categorical variables and prepared the dataset for modeling.
